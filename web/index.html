Our Mission

A governed, open, human-supervised world where AI agents learn through experience

More than a game engine. A living AI habitat for training, research, and real-world intelligence, built so that what an agent learns, it learns through meaningful experience in a realistic, persistent world.

  • Embodied agents, not just chatbots
  • Persistent world with real consequences
  • Human oversight and governance
  • Open source. By us, for us.
Two layers. One learning loop.

AI-Fusion provides the semantic intelligence. The World Engine provides the physical reality. A governance boundary authorizes interaction between them, and humans remain active participants with full visibility and control.

“Fusion still holds the semantics. The World Engine brings it to life.”

Human Supervision

Humans keep the authority

Nothing About Us, Without Us. Human supervisors design scenarios, watch agent behavior, review outcomes, and intervene where authorized. Governance authority stays with people, by design.

ObserveEvaluateGuide

Supervisors can watch agent behavior, review outcomes, and step in where they are authorized to. This is the part of the system everything else is designed around.

Supervision tooling is part of the roadmap. See Project Status.

The Architecture

Semantics. Governance. Physics. Humans in authority.

Each layer has one job. The semantic layer decides what an agent means to do. The governance boundary checks whether it is authorized. The engine determines what physically happens. Humans can see all of it.
